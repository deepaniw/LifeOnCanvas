My Art

Turning moments into colour

Painting has been one of my favourite creative pursuits over the years. I enjoy exploring colour, texture and light and bringing the things that inspire me to life on canvas.

Each painting is a small expression of imagination, joy and the beauty I see around me.

Where the Birches Dream

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24x24 canvas.
In a quiet grove touched by autumn’s light, birch trees drift between wakefulness and dream. Their golden leaves shimmer like thoughts of peace, inviting the viewer into a moment of stillness and wonder.

Birch at Dusk

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24x24 canvas.
Created with acrylic and modeling paste, this piece reflects that peaceful moment when the day softens into evening and the birch tree glows with the last touch of sunlight. A mix of warmth, texture, and calm — one of my favorite moments to paint.

Whispers of Spring

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24x24 canvas.
While the world outside turns to shades of gold and rust, I found myself painting memories of spring. ‘Whispers of Spring’ is a celebration of hope, color, and the beauty waiting just beyond winter’s edge.

Mirage of Seasons

Acrylic on a 24x24 canvas.
In this piece, I wanted to capture the fleeting beauty of change — the way one season fades into another, like a dream dissolving in light. The warmth of autumn, the memory of summer, and the cool whispers of winter all seem to merge at the horizon, creating a mirage of time and color. It’s a reminder that every ending holds the glow of a new beginning.

The Little Tree

A vibrant, expressive landscape featuring a solitary tree with fiery red foliage reflected across shimmering turquoise waters. The dreamy pastel sky and bold, textured brushstrokes create a beautiful contrast of warmth and coolness, capturing a sense of serenity, mystery, and quiet reflection.

About the Artist

A Creative Journey

I am an Ottawa-based artist who finds joy in creating with colour. Painting gives me a wonderful way to slow down, observe the world and express what I feel through art.

My creative interests extend beyond painting, and I enjoy many different forms of making and creating. Through Life on Canvas, I hope to share my paintings and a little of my creative journey with others.
