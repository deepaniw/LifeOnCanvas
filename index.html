My Art

Turning moments into colour

Painting has been one of my favourite creative pursuits over the years. I enjoy exploring colour, texture and light and bringing the things that inspire me to Art by Deepani.

Each painting is a small expression of imagination, joy and the beauty I see around me.

Where the Birches Dream

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24"x24" canvas.
In a quiet grove touched by autumn’s light, birch trees drift between wakefulness and dream. Their golden leaves shimmer like thoughts of peace, inviting the viewer into a moment of stillness and wonder.

Birch at Dusk

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24"x24" canvas.
Created with acrylic and modeling paste, this piece reflects that peaceful moment when the day softens into evening and the birch tree glows with the last touch of sunlight. A mix of warmth, texture, and calm — one of my favorite moments to paint.

Whispers of Spring

[SOLD] Acrylic on a 24"x24" canvas.

Mirage of Seasons

Acrylic on a 24"x24" canvas.
In this piece, I wanted to capture the fleeting beauty of change — the way one season fades into another, like a dream dissolving in light. The warmth of autumn, the memory of summer, and the cool whispers of winter all seem to merge at the horizon, creating a mirage of time and color. It’s a reminder that every ending holds the glow of a new beginning.

About the Artist

A Creative Journey


“Reflections of true human feelings expressed through brush strokes mingled with beautiful colors”

Originally from Sri Lanka and now based in Ottawa, Canada, I have been passionate about art since childhood. Inspired by my late father’s creative legacy, I find joy in exploring colour, texture, and emotion through abstract and landscape paintings. Painting gives me a wonderful way to slow down, observe the world and express what I feel through art.

My work has received recognition, including having my painting "Sailboats" featured as the cover photo for the Canadian Artist Association Facebook group in April 2023, and selections for the CAME (Canadian Association for Medical Education) Foundation Art Auction in 2024. Also my artworks are being displayed in the Rideau-Rockcliffe Family Medicine center.

My creative interests extend beyond painting, and I enjoy many different forms of making and creating. Through Art by Deepani, I invite you to share in my creative journey, where every painting tells a story and every colour holds a feeling.
